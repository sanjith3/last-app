Turf Owner Agreement

Last updated: February 2026

1. Partnership Overview

This agreement governs the relationship between turf facility owners ("Owner") and TurfZone Technologies ("Platform"). By listing your turf on our platform, you agree to the following terms.

2. Commission & Fees

  • TurfZone charges a 5% commission on each confirmed booking.
  • GST (18%) is applicable on the commission amount.
  • A platform fee of ₹10 per booking may apply.
  • All fees are deducted automatically before payout.

3. Payouts

  • Owner payouts are processed weekly (every Monday for the previous week's bookings).
  • Payouts are credited to the registered bank account via NEFT/IMPS.
  • Owners must provide valid bank account details and verify them.
  • Minimum payout threshold: ₹100.

4. Listing Requirements

  • Accurate facility information (size, surface type, amenities).
  • Clear, recent photographs of the facility.
  • Accurate pricing and slot availability.
  • Compliance with local regulations and safety standards.

5. Owner Responsibilities

  • Maintain the facility in the condition described in the listing.
  • Honor all confirmed bookings.
  • Ensure the facility is available and in playable condition at the booked time.
  • Respond to customer inquiries within 2 hours during business hours.
  • Maintain adequate safety equipment and first aid supplies.

6. Cancellation by Owner

If an owner cancels a confirmed booking:

  • The customer receives a full refund.
  • Repeated cancellations (>3 per month) may result in listing suspension.
  • Compensation credits may be awarded to affected customers.

7. Customer Privacy

Owners must respect customer privacy. Customer phone numbers are masked through our platform calling system. Owners must not:

  • Contact customers outside the platform.
  • Share customer information with third parties.
  • Use customer data for unsolicited marketing.

8. Dispute Resolution

Disputes between owners and customers are handled through TurfZone's dispute resolution system. Both parties can raise tickets, and our team will investigate and resolve within 48 hours.

9. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. Upon termination:

  • All pending bookings will be honored.
  • Outstanding payouts will be settled within 15 business days.
  • The listing will be removed from the platform.

10. Contact

For owner partnership inquiries: partners@turfzone.com
