Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

1. Overview

This Cancellation Policy applies to all bookings made through the TrufSpot platform. Depending on when you cancel, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund. Turf owners also have responsibilities when they need to cancel a booking.

2. User Cancellations

2.1 Cancellation by User (Player)

If you need to cancel a booking, the refund amount depends on the time remaining before the slot start time:

Cancellation Time Refund Amount
More than 24 hours before slot Full refund (minus platform fee ₹5 + GST)
Between 6 and 24 hours before slot 50% refund (minus platform fee ₹5 + GST)
Less than 6 hours before slot No refund
No-show (user does not arrive) No refund

Note: Platform fees and GST are non-refundable in all cases.

2.2 How to Cancel

To cancel a booking:

  1. Go to "My Bookings" in the app.
  2. Select the booking you wish to cancel.
  3. Tap "Cancel Booking" and follow the instructions.

Cancellation requests are processed immediately. Refunds will be credited to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

3. Turf Owner Cancellations

3.1 Cancellation by Owner

If a turf owner cancels a confirmed booking, the user will receive a full refund (including platform fees). Additionally, the owner may be subject to penalties:

  • First cancellation within 30 days: Warning
  • Second cancellation within 30 days: ₹200 penalty deducted from payout
  • Third cancellation within 30 days: Turf temporarily suspended

TrufSpot may also offer affected users a credit (₹50 wallet) as a goodwill gesture.

3.2 Owner-Initiated Cancellation Process

Owners can cancel a booking from their dashboard. A reason must be provided. Users will be notified immediately via push notification and email.

4. Refund Processing

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method (credit card, UPI, wallet, etc.).
  • It may take 5-7 business days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your bank.
  • If you paid using credits/wallet, refunds are credited back to your TrufSpot wallet instantly.

5. Force Majeure

In case of unforeseen circumstances (natural disasters, government restrictions, etc.), TrufSpot may cancel bookings and issue full refunds at its discretion. We will notify affected users as soon as possible.

6. Disputes

If you believe a cancellation or refund was handled incorrectly, please contact our support team within 7 days at support@turfspotx.com. Include your booking ID and reason for dispute. We will investigate and respond within 3 business days.
